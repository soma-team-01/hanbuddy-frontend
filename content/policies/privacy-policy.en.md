# HanBuddy Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-09-07
Effective date: 2026-09-20

Zero One (the “Company”) values HanBuddy users’ personal information and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information is processed and why, who receives or processes it, and users’ rights.

## 1. Personal Information Processed

The Company may process the following information when providing the Service.

| Category                         | Information                                                                                                                                              | Collection method                                                |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google login                     | Google account identifier, email, name, profile image                                                                                                    | Received from Google when the user chooses Google login          |
| Common profile                   | Nickname, nationality, date of birth, profile image                                                                                                      | Entered during registration or profile editing                   |
| Tourist contact                  | Preferred contact method, contact identifier or phone number                                                                                             | Entered by the Tourist during registration or profile editing    |
| Buddy application and operations | Phone number, application details, introduction, activity experience and qualification evidence, activity information and images, settlement information | Submitted by Buddies or generated during review and settlement   |
| Bookings and activities          | Booking number, activity, schedule, participant count, requests, cancellations, refunds, Buddy–Tourist messages, reviews                                 | Generated through booking, activity and support use              |
| Payment                          | Order number, amount, currency, discounts, payment method type, provider transaction identifier, authorization, cancellation and refund status           | Received from payment providers such as Toss Payments and PayPal |
| Support and safety               | Inquiry, reported person, submitted messages, photos, videos, receipts, attempted calls and results                                                      | Submitted by users or generated during dispute handling          |
| Service use                      | Access time, IP address, browser and device information, cookies, usage, error and security logs                                                         | Automatically generated during use                               |
| Marketing (optional)             | Email, nickname, marketing consent and withdrawal records                                                                                                | Optional user consent                                            |

The Company does not directly store full card numbers, card passwords or payment account passwords. Sensitive information and resident registration numbers are not processed without legal grounds or separate explicit consent.

## 2. Purposes

Personal information is processed for:

1. Registration, identity and age checks, login, account security and misuse prevention.
2. Buddy eligibility review, activity listing, display, operation and settlement.
3. Activity search, booking confirmation, payment, cancellation, refunds and communication between booking parties.
4. Messages, notifications, translation, maps and address search.
5. Inquiries, safety reports, dispute resolution, remedies and legal obligations.
6. Failure analysis, security, quality improvement and statistics.
7. Event and promotion information where the user gives optional consent.

## 3. Retention and Use Periods

Information is destroyed promptly when no longer needed, except records retained under applicable law:

| Record                                   | Retention                                                                                                        | Basis or purpose                                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contracts and withdrawal                 | 5 years                                                                                                          | Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc. |
| Payments and supply of goods or services | 5 years                                                                                                          | Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc. |
| Consumer complaints and disputes         | 3 years                                                                                                          | Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc. |
| Display and advertising                  | 6 months                                                                                                         | Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc. |
| Consent and acceptance of terms          | Until applicable transaction and dispute retention periods end, even after account closure or consent withdrawal | Evidence of consent and dispute handling                    |

Other profile and account information is normally processed until account closure; marketing information until consent withdrawal or closure. For ongoing bookings, settlements or disputes, only necessary information is stored separately until processing and statutory retention periods end.

## 4. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not disclose information without consent or legal grounds. Once a booking is confirmed, it provides the following necessary information to the booked activity’s Buddy within the Tourist’s consent:

| Recipient                           | Purpose                                                                                           | Information                                                                                                | Retention and use                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Buddy providing the booked activity | Participant identification, preparation, schedule and location communication, safety and disputes | Tourist nickname, profile image, participant count, requests, preferred contact method and contact details | Until activity and dispute purposes are fulfilled. Buddies must promptly delete separately stored information. |

Nicknames and profile images are disclosed equally for Buddies and Tourists. Member-posted activities and reviews may also be displayed to Service users. Buddy phone numbers and other contact information submitted for application review are not disclosed or provided to Tourists or general users.

Tourists may refuse disclosure consent but cannot book because necessary activity communication would be impossible. Disclosure without consent may occur where legally permitted for urgent protection of life or body, lawful investigative requests or similar grounds.

| Recipient and contact               | Countries                                               | Information                                                                                                     | Purpose                                                                    | Timing and method                                      | Retention                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Google LLC (Google Privacy Inquiry) | United States and countries hosting Google data centers | Login requests and responses, Google identifier, email, name, profile image, address queries and technical logs | Login, maps, address search and security                                   | Encrypted network transmission when using the feature  | Google policy and Section 3 retention periods    |

## 7. Destruction

1. Information is promptly destroyed when its retention period ends or purpose is fulfilled.
2. Information legally required to be retained is kept separately and not used for other purposes.
3. Electronic files are deleted using methods that make recovery difficult; paper records are shredded or incinerated.

## 8. Rights of Users and Legal Representatives

1. Users may exercise statutory rights including access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, consent withdrawal and data transmission requests.
2. Requests may be made through profile or account settings or contact@hanbuddy.kr.
3. After verifying identity or lawful representation, the Company responds within the statutory period and communicates results. Legal restrictions are explained with appeal instructions.
4. Registration by people under 19 is not permitted. If minors’ information is collected in error, it is verified and promptly deleted.

## 9. Security Measures

The Company implements necessary technical and organizational measures, including minimal access privileges, authentication and access control, encryption in transit and storage, access-log retention and review, vulnerability management, backups and staff training.

## 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

1. Cookies and similar storage maintain login, language settings, security and Service improvement.
2. Essential cookies are necessary for the Service. If analytics or advertising cookies are introduced, their purposes, providers and retention periods are separately disclosed and necessary consent obtained.
3. Users may delete or block cookies in browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent login and other features.

## 11. Automated Decisions

The Company currently does not make decisions significantly affecting users’ rights or obligations solely by automated means. If introduced, criteria, effects and ways to object or request explanations will be disclosed here.
