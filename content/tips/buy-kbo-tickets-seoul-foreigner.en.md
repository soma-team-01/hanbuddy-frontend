A foreigner can buy KBO tickets for a Seoul game online through the home club's ticketing partner or in person at the stadium on game day. Online booking usually requires verification with a Korean mobile number, so short-term visitors often rely on the stadium: in 2026 the LG Twins and Doosan Bears sold a limited number of same-day seats to foreigners who showed a passport. Each answer below stands on its own.

_Facts checked November 6, 2026. Club rules are from the 2026 season; recheck them before buying for 2027._

## Booking online

### Which site should I use, Interpark or Ticketlink?

The one the home club uses. In 2026:

| Club | Site | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LG Twins | Ticketlink ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)) | 11:00, seven days before the game |
| Doosan Bears | NOL Ticket, formerly Interpark ([EpicKor](https://www.epickor.com/blog/233), secondhand) | Seven days before |
| Kiwoom Heroes | Interpark ([Seoul Facilities Corporation](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/opensquare/bbs/bbsMsgDetail.do?bcd=faq&msg_seq=184)) | Seven days before |

The clubs link to the foreign-visitor versions, Ticketlink Global and NOL World ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260303/how-to-enjoy-baseball-the-korean-way)).

### Do I need a Korean phone number to book online?

Usually, yes. The Korea Times notes that some ticketing apps require a Korean phone number, which makes online purchase difficult for tourists ([source](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260303/how-to-enjoy-baseball-the-korean-way)). A fan guide on note.com (as of April 2026) lists that LG needs a Korean mobile number to reserve, and Doosan and Kiwoom need Korean SMS or i-PIN verification to register ([note.com](https://note.com/washicomp/n/n0516a6f7afc8?hl=en)). A tourist SIM or eSIM does not pass the check ([Korea Visit Guide](https://www.koreavisitguide.com/en/activities/book-kbo-baseball-tickets-guide)).

### I live in Korea. Does this apply to me?

Less so. The check is built around a Korean resident registration number or an Alien Registration Card linked to a domestic SIM ([Korea Baseball Guide](https://koreabaseballguide.com/how-to-buy-2026-kbo-postseason-and-korean-series-tickets-as-a-foreigner/)), so a resident with a Korean number in their own name can try the normal route.

### How many tickets can I book, and is there a fee?

For LG in 2026: up to 8 tickets per game, with a 1,000 won booking fee per ticket ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)).

### Who can help if the booking fails?

For LG games, LG lists the Ticketlink Global Call Center at 1644-3850 ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)).

## Buying at the stadium

### Can I buy tickets at the box office on game day?

Yes, if seats remain. At Gocheok Sky Dome, seats left from online sales go on sale on site from two hours before the game ([Seoul Facilities Corporation](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/opensquare/bbs/bbsMsgDetail.do?bcd=faq&msg_seq=184)). LG's 2026 ticket office opened 1 hour 30 minutes before weekday games and 2 hours before weekend games ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)). Game-day availability is not guaranteed, so arrive several hours early for popular games ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260303/how-to-enjoy-baseball-the-korean-way)).

### What is the foreigner ticket window?

A counter that sells a set number of same-day seats to buyers who cannot book through Korean online ticketing. LG calls its counter the Digital Assistance Window and lists it in its on-site guide for international visitors. In 2026 at Jamsil Baseball Stadium:

- **LG Twins:** Ticket Office No. 3, the Digital Assistance Window, on the left after Exit 5 of Sports Complex Station. Opens 90 minutes before weekday games and 2 hours before weekend and holiday games. Each base side had 10 Blue, 30 Red and 80 Green seats ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)).
- **Doosan Bears:** Ticket Office No. 1. Opens with the general box office and sells until 30 minutes after the start. Each base side had 20 Blue, 60 Red and 160 outfield seats ([note.com, citing Doosan](https://note.com/washicomp/n/n0516a6f7afc8?hl=en), secondhand).

Both sell first come, first served until the seats run out.

### Do I need my passport at the foreigner window?

Yes. LG requires a valid passport and sells up to 2 tickets per person ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)). Doosan requires a passport and sells 1 ticket per person, so each person in a group must bring their own ([note.com](https://note.com/washicomp/n/n0516a6f7afc8?hl=en)).

### Does this work for the postseason too?

In 2026 it did. NOL's Korean Series page lists a dedicated window for seniors, people with disabilities and foreigners, limited to 200 tickets and open from two hours before the game to the end of the 8th inning ([NOL Ticket](https://nol.yanolja.com/ticket/genre/sports/postseason)).

## Season and venues

### Are Seoul teams playing now?

Not on November 6, 2026. The Korean Series between the Samsung Lions and KT Wiz is being played in Daegu and Suwon, with games scheduled through November 11 ([KBO](https://eng.koreabaseball.com/Schedule/DailySchedule.aspx)). Seoul home games return in the 2027 season; the opening date was not on the KBO schedule when checked.

### Where will LG Twins and Doosan Bears play in 2027?

At Jamsil Olympic Stadium, for the five seasons from 2027 to 2031, converted to an 18,000-seat ballpark with up to 30,000 seats for major games. Jamsil Baseball Stadium is being rebuilt as a 30,000-seat dome due in March 2032 ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/sports/20240709/lg-twins-and-doosan-bears-to-temporarily-play-at-olympic-main-stadium-for-5-seasons)). The window numbers above are for the old stadium, so check the club page for the new one.

### Where do the Kiwoom Heroes play?

At Gocheok Sky Dome, where the club runs its own ticket sales ([Seoul Facilities Corporation](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/opensquare/bbs/bbsMsgDetail.do?bcd=faq&msg_seq=184)).

## Other options

### What if I cannot risk a sellout?

Book a hosted experience that includes the ticket; Korea Visit Guide lists this as one of three workarounds ([source](https://www.koreavisitguide.com/en/activities/book-kbo-baseball-tickets-guide)). HanBuddy is one option: it connects travelers and foreign residents with Korean local buddies, and its game nights include the match ticket and stadium food ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)). Payment is online with PayPal, and bookings close 24 hours before the start. See [Open-Air KBO Baseball Night](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/2), [Indoor Dome KBO Baseball Night](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/1), and [How to Watch a KBO Game in Seoul With a Local](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/watch-kbo-baseball-game-seoul-with-local).

### Is there any club where foreigners can book advance tickets from abroad?

The KT Wiz, in Suwon rather than Seoul. Its Wizzap app takes foreigner registration by email and overseas card payment, and the note.com guide calls it the only such route ([note.com](https://note.com/washicomp/n/n0516a6f7afc8?hl=en)).
