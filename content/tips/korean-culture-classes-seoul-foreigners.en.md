Yes, foreigners can take traditional Korean culture classes in Seoul in English. One-day classes in calligraphy, minhwa (Korean folk painting), knot and paper crafts, and cooking run in studios, museums and culture centers, mostly in Jongno-gu (Insa-dong, Bukchon), Itaewon and the Ewha Womans University area. The classes listed here mostly last 1.5 to 3 hours, several are aimed at beginners, and most send you home with something you made. The steps below take you from picking a class type to booking. Facts were checked on November 7, 2026; schedules and prices change, so confirm on each provider's page.

## Step 1: Pick the class type by what you want to take home

| Class type | What you do | What you take home | Typical length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calligraphy (*seoye*) | Practice brush strokes, then write your name or a phrase in Hangeul, the Korean alphabet | A hanging scroll or card | About 1.5–2 hours |
| Minhwa folk painting | Color a pre-drawn traditional design such as a tiger and magpie or peonies | A finished painting | About 2–3 hours |
| Knot and paper crafts | Make a small item with *maedeup* (Korean knotting) or *hanji* (traditional paper) | A keychain, bracelet or accessory | Varies by item |
| Cooking | Cook two or more dishes and eat them | A meal and the recipes | About 1.5 hours or more |

Calligraphy and minhwa suit people who want a souvenir and a quiet, seated activity. Cooking suits people who would rather eat what they make. On price, the Bukchon museum and workshop programs below list the lowest starting fees (from 3,000 won at Gahoe Museum, plus 5,000 won admission), while private studio classes on booking platforms run to ₩120,000–150,000.

## Step 2: Check four things on the listing

1. **Language of instruction.** Look for "English" stated for the class itself, not just an English booking page.
2. **Group size.** A smaller group leaves the teacher fewer people to help at once. K-Harmony's minhwa class in Itaewon takes up to 6 participants per session, and an Insa-dong minhwa experience on Airbnb takes up to 10.
3. **Age and experience.** Both of those minhwa classes require guests aged 10 and over and are aimed at beginners.
4. **Cancellation terms.** These vary a lot (see Step 4).

## Step 3: Choose a venue

### Calligraphy

- **Feel So Good Calli Studio, Insa-dong:** a 1.5-hour hanging-scroll class taught in English, a few minutes from Anguk Station. Brushes, ink and paper are included ([Trazy listing](https://www.trazy.com/experience/detail/korean-calligraphy-hanging-scroll-making-class-insadong)).
- **Seoul Culture Lounge, Seoul Tourism Plaza:** runs calligraphy on Fridays among its programs for foreign nationals traveling or living in Korea (see below).

### Minhwa folk painting

- **K-Harmony, Itaewon:** about 150 minutes in English, near Itaewon Station on Line 6, with materials and gift packaging included ([Trazy listing](https://www.trazy.com/experience/detail/korean-traditional-minhwa-painting-itaewon)).
- **Yoana's studio, Seoul:** a 2-hour minhwa and ink painting class hosted in Korean and English by an Insa-dong folk painting lecturer, from ₩120,000 per guest ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/experiences/6954372)).
- **Alloc Seoul and Minhwa Gain:** both offer classes in English, according to [The Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/lifestyle/koreanheritage/20260114/painting-peonies-and-tigers-where-to-learn-minhwa-in-korea) (January 2026). Alloc Seoul uses acrylic paints instead of powdered pigments; Minhwa Gain's studio is near Ewha Womans University. Both list details on Instagram.
- **Gahoe Museum, Bukchon (52 Bukchon-ro):** one-day minhwa workshops on paper, wood panels or fans; its intensive courses require fluency in Korean (same Korea Times article). VisitKorea lists activity fees of 3,000–30,000 won plus 5,000 won admission, closed Mondays, and a Korean-only website.

### Crafts

[VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=139389), the Korea Tourism Organization's site, lists Bukchon workshops (page last updated October 28, 2025):

- **Donglim Knot Workshop:** knotted keychain charms and bracelets for 10,000–12,000 won; inquiries in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese; closed Mondays.
- **Bukchon Traditional Crafts Experience:** dyeing, hanji accessories and knot bracelets, 10,000–320,000 won depending on the program; inquiries in Korean and English.

### Cooking

- **NOW COOKING Studio, Seodaemun-gu:** bulgogi and kimbap, about 1 hour of cooking and 30 minutes of eating, guide language Korean/English, ₩150,000 per person, on weekends at 17:30 and 19:00 ([Seoul Pass listing](http://www.seoultravelpass.com/index.php/en/products/1332-seoul-cooking-class-for-korean-food-experience-with-now-cooking-studio)). The listing gives two different minimum group sizes, so confirm before booking.
- **Seoul Culture Lounge:** a Traditional Korean Cooking Experience on Wednesdays.

### A culture center with several class types

[Seoul Culture Lounge](https://www.seoulculturelounge.com/?lang=en) on the 11th floor of Seoul Tourism Plaza (85 Cheonggyecheon-ro, Jongno-gu) runs a weekly rotation including cooking, mother-of-pearl craft, pansori (traditional narrative singing), calligraphy and traditional dance, Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00–17:00. Bookings open at 10:00 KST seven days ahead, a voucher must be bought before registering, and participants must be 14 or older.

### With a local buddy

[HanBuddy](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/about) is a platform that connects travelers and foreign residents in Korea with local buddies who host small-group experiences. Two of its classes are taught by artist Damdamhi in Korean, English and Japanese, at a studio in Jamsil (3rd floor, Rose Apartment Shopping Center A):

- [Calligraphy](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/8): line practice (40 min), phrase practice (50 min), then writing on a *jokja* hanging scroll with your seal (20 min).
- [Minhwa](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/7): choose a peony, magpie-and-tiger or *bok* character painting, about 2–3 hours, using prepared outlines.

Equipment is included. These classes have no reviews yet. Check each page for open dates.

## Step 4: Book and read the cancellation rules

- K-Harmony: full refund 3 days before; non-refundable 2 days before or less.
- Feel So Good Calli: full refund 3 days before, 50% at 2 days, none from 1 day before.
- Airbnb minhwa class: full refund if cancelled at least 1 day before.
- Seoul Culture Lounge: no refund for same-day cancellation or no-shows; arriving more than 15 minutes late counts as a cancellation.

## Step 5: If your first choice is full

1. Try another day at the same venue.
2. Switch to a venue in the same class type from Step 3.
3. Call a Bukchon craft workshop on the inquiry number listed by VisitKorea and ask about same-day space.
4. Ask the 1330 Korea Travel Hotline (+82-2-1330), listed by VisitKorea, for help in English.

## Winter tips

All of these classes are indoors. Several Bukchon workshops shorten hours to 10:00–17:00 from November to February, and Gahoe Museum does so from December to February (VisitKorea). For an evening plan with a local, see [How to Watch a KBO Game in Seoul With a Local](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/watch-kbo-baseball-game-seoul-with-local).
