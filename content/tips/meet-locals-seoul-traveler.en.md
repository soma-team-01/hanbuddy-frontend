Yes. A solo traveler can meet locals and make friends in Seoul by joining a small-group experience hosted by a Korean local, where a shared game or class does the introducing for you. On HanBuddy, a local buddy hosts experiences such as a KBO baseball night, an FC Seoul matchday or a Hangeul calligraphy class and goes with you from start to finish. Guests come solo, as couples and with friends, and everyone sits together.

## What is a local buddy?

A buddy is a Korean local who hosts a small-group experience in English and goes with you, from sorting out the tickets to explaining what is happening around you ([HanBuddy](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/about)). A buddy is not a private guide hired for a day of sightseeing: you book a specific experience, and the buddy is with you for that experience. So far the buddies are HanBuddy's own team and licensed tour guides. HanBuddy checks every buddy before they host, and a new buddy's first experience is always hosted together with the team ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)).

## What does HanBuddy include, and who is it for?

HanBuddy connects travelers and foreign residents in Korea with local buddies. You pick a date, pay online, and a real person answers your questions before the day.

- **Game nights:** the admission ticket and a meal are included, and your buddy meets you an hour before the start, enters with you, teaches the cheering songs and explains the game.
- **Culture classes:** all equipment is included, and you take home what you make.
- **Who it suits:** solo travelers who want company, first-timers at a Korean stadium, and anyone who wants to spend time with Koreans rather than only see the sights. No Korean is needed. Game nights are for guests aged 19 and older.

Since June 2026 HanBuddy has hosted 8 game nights for 20 guests, with an average rating of 4.9 out of 5 from 15 guest surveys (June to October 2026, [About](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/about)).

## Which experiences can I join?

- **[Open-Air KBO Baseball Night](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/2)** at Jamsil: four steps from meeting your buddy (15 minutes) to watching the game together (about 180 minutes). Hosted in Korean and English.
- **[Indoor Dome KBO Baseball Night](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/1)** at Gocheok Sky Dome: the same plan indoors, without worrying about the weather.
- **[K League Football Matchday](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/6)** at Seoul World Cup Stadium: food from the plaza food trucks, FC Seoul cheering songs, about 120 minutes of match. Hosted in Korean and English.
- **[Hangeul calligraphy class](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/8)** in Jamsil with artist Damdamhi: about 110 minutes; you write your name or a phrase on a *jokja* (traditional hanging scroll). *Hangeul* is the Korean alphabet.
- **[Minhwa folk painting class](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/7)** in Jamsil with Damdamhi: about 2 to 3 hours; you paint a peony, a magpie and tiger, or the character for *bok* (good fortune). *Minhwa* is Korean folk painting.

Both classes are taught in Korean, English and Japanese.

## How will I actually make friends if I come alone?

Your buddy is the first local you meet, and the evening is built around talking with them: the buddy guides you through entry, rules and cheering and chats with you along the way ([experience page](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/2)). Because everyone sits together, any other guests on your date are part of the same group. HanBuddy hosts this way because its first guest said the game alone was not enough and wanted to meet the Koreans beside them ([About](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/about)).

Guests have written:

- "It genuinely felt like spending time with friends rather than being a customer on a typical tour." (baseball night, September 2026)
- "The guide was nice and it was like being with friends." (football night, August 2026)
- "A great and fun way to experience baseball with some lovely, kind people!" (baseball night, September 2026)

## Do I need to speak Korean or know the sport?

No. Your buddy explains the rules and teaches you the cheering songs as you go ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)). At the classes, beginners work from prepared outlines (minhwa) or start with line practice (calligraphy), so no art experience is needed. For more detail, read [how to watch a KBO game with a local](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/watch-kbo-baseball-game-seoul-with-local) and [how to take a culture class in English](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/korean-culture-classes-seoul-foreigners).

## Can I hire a local friend instead?

HanBuddy works per experience rather than by the hour: you book the game night or class you want, and a buddy hosts it and goes with you. If you would rather go with a Korean friend you already know, the same events are open to you both; the guides to [buying KBO tickets](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/buy-kbo-tickets-seoul-foreigner) and [watching a K League match](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/watch-k-league-football-match-seoul-tourist) cover booking yourselves.

## How do I book, and what if plans change?

1. Choose a date on the experience page and pay with PayPal. For another method, such as a Korean bank transfer, tap "Need another payment method?" on the booking page.
2. Add any dietary restrictions under Special Requests.
3. Book at least 24 hours ahead; bookings close 24 hours before the start.

Before buddy approval you can cancel for a full refund. After approval, you get a full refund at least 48 hours before the start, 50% from 24 to less than 48 hours before, and nothing within 24 hours. If your buddy cancels, you get a full refund ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)). The meeting point is on each experience page, and HanBuddy confirms the exact spot by message before the day.

## When can I go, and what if my date is full?

Check game dates on the official [KBO schedule](https://eng.koreabaseball.com/Schedule/DailySchedule.aspx) and the [FC Seoul site](https://www.fcseoul.com), then the open dates on the HanBuddy page. From 2027 to 2031 the LG Twins and Doosan Bears play at Jamsil Olympic Stadium while their ballpark is rebuilt ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/sports/20240709/lg-twins-and-doosan-bears-to-temporarily-play-at-olympic-main-stadium-for-5-seasons)). If your date is full, choose another date or another HanBuddy experience; the classes do not depend on a league calendar. For general travel help, the Korea Tourism Organization's [1330 Korea Travel Helpline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632&menuSn=454) answers by phone (1330 in Korea, +82-2-1330 from overseas) and chat.
