It depends on the sport. For baseball in any weather, pick Gocheok Sky Dome, the KBO's only indoor ballpark and a 3-minute walk from Guil Station. For football, pick Seoul World Cup Stadium. From 2027, pick the temporary Jamsil ballpark to see the LG Twins or Doosan Bears. HanBuddy runs game nights at Gocheok Sky Dome and Seoul World Cup Stadium, and has hosted KBO nights at Jamsil Baseball Stadium, where a Korean local buddy goes with you, with the match ticket and stadium food included.

## Which stadium should I pick?

| Stadium | Pick it if | Getting there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gocheok Sky Dome | You want baseball whatever the weather | Line 1, Guil Station Exit 2 |
| Seoul World Cup Stadium | You want K League football with FC Seoul | Line 6, World Cup Stadium Station Exit 2 |
| Jamsil Olympic Stadium (2027–2031) | You want the LG Twins or Doosan Bears | Lines 2 and 9, Sports Complex Station |

The Korea Times describes Gocheok as the league's only indoor baseball stadium ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260303/how-to-enjoy-baseball-the-korean-way), March 2026). Seoul World Cup Stadium's roof covers 90% of its 66,704 seats ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)).

## How do I get to Gocheok Sky Dome by subway?

Take Line 1 to Guil Station and use Exit 2; the dome is about 150 m ahead, roughly 3 minutes on foot. The dome's operator also lists Gaebong Station Exit 2 (about 15 minutes' walk) and Guro Station Exit 2 (about 20 minutes), and suggests Guro for visitors from Seoul, Suwon or Anyang and Gaebong for those from Incheon or Bucheon to avoid crowds ([Seoul Facilities Corporation](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/skydome/introduce/location.jsp)). Do not plan to drive: on game days the organiser takes over the car park ([parking](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/skydome/guidance/parking.jsp)).

## Where do Seoul baseball teams play in 2027?

Jamsil Baseball Stadium hosted its last regular-season game on October 12, 2026 and will be demolished for a new dome ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/sports/twins-bears-to-meet-in-kbo-wild-card-in-battle-of-jamsil-rivals/12917467)). From 2027 to 2031 the LG Twins and Doosan Bears play at Jamsil Olympic Stadium, converted to an 18,000-capacity ballpark with extra seats for up to 30,000 at major games ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/sports/20240709/lg-twins-and-doosan-bears-to-temporarily-play-at-olympic-main-stadium-for-5-seasons)). Sports Complex Station (Lines 2 and 9) serves the sports complex that includes it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Complex_station_(Seoul))). The Kiwoom Heroes stay at Gocheok. Gates and ticket windows at the temporary ballpark are new, so check the club sites before the season, and see the [KBO ticket guide](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/buy-kbo-tickets-seoul-foreigner) for buying as a foreigner.

## Can you bring your own food and beer into a baseball stadium in Seoul?

Yes for food; drinks have limits. The KBO's SAFE Campaign rules for every ballpark ([KBO](https://www.koreabaseball.com/Kbo/BusinessAndEvent/Safe.aspx), checked November 9, 2026):

- Food is allowed unless banned, but hot soups and strong-smelling food may be refused.
- No glass, no PET over 1 litre in total, no cans, except unopened drinks up to one PET bottle and two cans per person.
- One bag up to 45 × 45 × 20 cm plus one shopping bag up to 30 × 50 × 12 cm.
- No coolers, boxes, mats, folding chairs, frozen water bottles or cooking equipment.

Clubs may add their own rules. At Gocheok, alcohol of 6% or less is allowed, and food cannot be brought in on re-entry ([Seoul Facilities Corporation FAQ](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/skydome/community/faq.jsp)).

## What food can you eat at a Korean baseball stadium?

Fried chicken, pizza and tteokbokki (spicy simmered rice cakes) are common, and beer is sold throughout the stands ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260303/how-to-enjoy-baseball-the-korean-way)). Fans call ballpark food _yapu_, from _yagu_ (baseball) and "food"; in a Korea Professional Sports Association survey of 9,321 visitors, concessions scored 4.48 out of 5 ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/others/20260505/came-for-the-food-baseball-was-a-bonus-inside-koreas-ballpark-food-culture), May 2026). Fans list kimchi noodle soup among Jamsil's dishes (Korea Times, May 2026).

On HanBuddy nights, buddies often get fried chicken and beer; at Gocheok, fried shrimp in cream sauce is a guest favourite. The meal is included, and dietary needs go under Special Requests when you book ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)).

## When should I arrive, and what should I bring?

The Korea Times recommends arriving at least two hours before a baseball game; HanBuddy meets you one hour before. Bring a bag within the size limit and your passport if you plan to buy at a foreigner ticket window. If you sit in a cheering zone, expect to stand when your team bats (Korea Times, March 2026). Going with a Korean friend instead? See [how to watch a KBO game with a local](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/tips/watch-kbo-baseball-game-seoul-with-local).
