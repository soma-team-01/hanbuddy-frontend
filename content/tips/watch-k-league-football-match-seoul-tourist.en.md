## When is the season, and how do I find FC Seoul's home games?

The K League season begins in late February or early March and runs through November, with promotion-relegation playoffs held in December in some years ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260813/pro-football-players-association-calls-on-top-league-to-switch-to-fall-spring-schedule)). In K League 1, twelve teams play 38 matches each. After 33 rounds the league splits into "Final A" (top six) and "Final B" (bottom six), and each team plays the five others in its group once more ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/sports/new-k-league-1-season-to-kick-off-feb-28/12361317)).

To find a date, open the **Matches** menu on the FC Seoul site ([fcseoul.com schedule](https://www.fcseoul.com/matches/schresultList)), choose the KLEAGUE 1 tab, and look for games at Seoul World Cup Stadium. Check the kickoff time again in the week of the game, since it can move.

## Can I buy FC Seoul tickets online as a foreigner?

Only if you can pass Korean mobile phone verification. FC Seoul sells online through its own site and Ticketlink ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)). The fan guide sooutof notes that you can register on the club site as a foreigner, but phone verification is still required ([sooutof](https://sooutof.com/fc-seoul-stadium-guide-k-league/)). K League United, writing about the league as a whole, says that without a Korean phone number buying online in advance is "effectively impossible" ([K League United](http://www.kleagueunited.com/2026/01/buying-tickets-and-football-guide-in.html)).

Foreign residents with a Korean number can book like locals. Home-game tickets open at 18:00 five days before the match ([FC Seoul tickets](https://www.fcseoul.com/tickets/reserveSingleTicket)), and online reservations close 3 hours before kickoff ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)). A short-term visitor should plan on the box office.

## How do I buy a ticket at the stadium on match day?

1. Arrive early. According to sooutof, the on-site booth opens 2 hours before kickoff, and the most convenient one is the North Ticket Office at the top of the large main stairs from the North Plaza.
2. Pay by card. K League United warns that cash may not be accepted and that a small processing fee, usually around 2,000 won, is added at the booth.
3. Keep the ticket. FC Seoul says a ticket bought at the booth on match day can be refunded only before kickoff ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)).

The stadium has 66,704 seats ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)). Prices depend on the section and the match. For a July 2026 game, the Korea Herald gave the regular on-site price for general admission or the supporters' section as 20,000 won ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10808387)). The club's [ticket page](https://www.fcseoul.com/tickets/reserveSingleTicket) lists current prices.

## Do I need my passport?

Bring it. The guides disagree: sooutof tells visitors to bring a passport, while K League United says tourists are not asked for identification at the booth. Foreigner-only promotions, when they run, ask for proof of nationality, so carrying your passport covers both cases.

## Are there discounts for foreigners?

Sometimes, as one-off events rather than a standing offer. For the July 26, 2026 home game, foreign nationals could buy a general admission or supporters' section ticket for 11,000 won with one free draft beer, showing a passport, residence card or international driving permit ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10808387)). For September 5, 2026, the Seoul Foreign Resident Center ran an "International Fans' Day" with a supporting-section ticket and a draft beer coupon for 11,000 won, sold at a foreigners-only booth near the North Gate third-floor ticket box ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10855505)). Look for new promotions in the notices on [fcseoul.com](https://www.fcseoul.com/en) before your game.

## How do I get to Seoul World Cup Stadium, and when should I arrive?

Take Subway Line 6 to World Cup Stadium Station; Exit 1 leads to the stadium and Peace Park ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cup_Stadium_station_(Seoul))). Gates open 2 hours before kickoff, and sooutof recommends arriving at least an hour early so you have time to buy a ticket and food.

## Where should I sit, and can I wear another team's shirt?

For the loudest atmosphere, sit in the north stand, which the club calls FC Seoul's hottest supporting seats ([FC Seoul tickets](https://www.fcseoul.com/tickets/reserveSingleTicket)). sooutof points to the lower tiers of blocks C and D there, where fans stand and cheer for the full 90 minutes. If you would rather sit, choose another section.

Do not wear the visiting team's shirt in home areas. The club marks several sections as FC Seoul cheering areas where opposing fans cannot enter, and there is a separate away section (FC Seoul tickets page). K League United suggests a main stand or neutral section if you have no team gear.

## What can I eat and drink?

The North Plaza has a food park with food trucks. Inside, sooutof says alcohol is sold only at the stadium's convenience stores, and plastic bottles over 600 ml are not allowed. The same guide says draft beer in plastic cups from the plaza trucks may be taken in, but to confirm with staff before buying. Korean stadiums allow drinking at your seat (K League United).
