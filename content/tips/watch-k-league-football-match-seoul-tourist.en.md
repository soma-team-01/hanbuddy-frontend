A tourist can watch a K League match in Seoul by going to an FC Seoul home game at Seoul World Cup Stadium and buying a ticket at the box office on match day. Online booking usually needs Korean phone verification, but games rarely sell out, so buying on the day is the practical route for a short visit. The answers below were checked on November 6, 2026, and say whether each fact comes from the club (official) or from fan guides and press (secondhand).

## Season and dates

### When does the K League season start and end?

It runs from late February or early March through November, with promotion-relegation playoffs sometimes held in December ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260813/pro-football-players-association-calls-on-top-league-to-switch-to-fall-spring-schedule)). The 2026 K League 1 season opened on February 28 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/sports/new-k-league-1-season-to-kick-off-feb-28/12361317)) and its last round is on December 6 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_K_League_1)).

### How does the season work?

Twelve teams play 38 matches each. After 33 rounds the top six ("Final A") and bottom six ("Final B") play five more games within their group (Korea JoongAng Daily, above).

## Tickets

### Can I buy FC Seoul tickets online as a foreigner?

Only if you can pass Korean phone verification. The club sells online through fcseoul.com and Ticketlink ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)). Secondhand guides say a foreigner can sign up on the club site but still needs mobile verification ([sooutof](https://sooutof.com/fc-seoul-stadium-guide-k-league/)), and that without a Korean number buying in advance is "effectively impossible" ([K League United](http://www.kleagueunited.com/2026/01/buying-tickets-and-football-guide-in.html)). Foreign residents with a Korean number can book like locals.

### Can I use Ticketlink Global in English without a Korean number?

We could not confirm that FC Seoul tickets are sold there, so do not plan around it. For a short visit, plan on the stadium box office instead.

### When do online tickets go on sale?

At 18:00 five days before each home game. Online booking closes 3 hours before kickoff ([FC Seoul tickets](https://www.fcseoul.com/tickets/reserveSingleTicket), [FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)).

### Can I buy at the stadium on match day?

Yes. A secondhand guide says the booth opens 2 hours before kickoff, and the North Ticket Office at the top of the main stairs from the North Plaza is the most convenient (sooutof). Expect to pay by card; cash may not be accepted, and booths add a small fee, usually around 2,000 won (K League United).

### Will it sell out?

Rarely. The stadium has 66,704 seats ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)). The biggest 2026 home crowd listed on Wikipedia was 35,729, on May 5 against FC Anyang ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FC_Seoul_season)).

### How much does a ticket cost?

It depends on the section and match. For a July 2026 game, the Korea Herald gave the regular on-site price for general admission or the supporters' section as 20,000 won ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10808387)). See the club's ticket page for current prices.

### Do I need my passport?

Sources disagree. sooutof says to bring it; K League United says tourists are not asked for ID at booths. Bring it anyway, because the 2026 foreigner promotions required ID.

### Are there discounts for foreigners?

On some dates. On July 26, 2026, foreign nationals paid 11,000 won with a free draft beer, showing a passport, residence card or international driving permit ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10808387)). On September 5, 2026, an "International Fans' Day" with the Seoul Foreign Resident Center offered the same price at a foreigners-only booth near the North Gate third-floor ticket box ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10855505)). These were one-off events; check FC Seoul's notices for your date.

### Can I get a refund?

For booth tickets, only before kickoff ([FC Seoul](https://www.fcseoul.com/en)).

## At the stadium

### How do I get to Seoul World Cup Stadium?

Take Subway Line 6 to World Cup Stadium Station, next to the stadium ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cup_Stadium_station_(Seoul))).

### Where is the cheering section?

The north stand, which the club describes as its hottest supporting section ([FC Seoul tickets](https://www.fcseoul.com/tickets/reserveSingleTicket)). In its lower C and D blocks, fans stand and cheer for 90 minutes (sooutof).

### Can I wear another team's shirt?

Not the visiting team's in home sections. The club marks several sections as FC Seoul cheering areas where away fans are not admitted (FC Seoul tickets page). K League United suggests a main stand or neutral section if you have no team gear.

### When should I arrive?

Gates open 2 hours before kickoff; about an hour early leaves time for food (sooutof).

### What can I eat and drink?

The North Plaza has a food park with food trucks. Inside, alcohol is sold only at convenience stores, and plastic bottles over 600 ml are not allowed (sooutof).

## Going with a local

### Can I go with a Korean local?

Yes. [HanBuddy](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/about), a platform that connects travelers and foreign residents with Korean local buddies, runs a [K League Football Matchday](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/6) at FC Seoul home games.

### What does HanBuddy's football night include?

The ticket and a meal. You meet your buddy an hour before kickoff, get food from the plaza food trucks, and learn FC Seoul cheering songs. It is hosted in Korean and English for guests aged 19 and older. HanBuddy hosted its first football night in August 2026, and since June 2026 it has run 8 game nights for 20 guests, rated 4.9 out of 5 on average across 15 guest surveys.

### How do booking and cancellation work?

Pay with PayPal; bookings close 24 hours before the start. After buddy approval, the refund is full at least 48 hours before, 50% from 24 to under 48 hours, and none within 24 hours ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)).
