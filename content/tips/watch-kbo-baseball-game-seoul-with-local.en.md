A foreigner can watch a KBO game in Seoul with a local by going with a Korean friend, booking a host-led game experience on a marketplace such as Airbnb Experiences, or booking a small-group game night on a local-buddy platform such as HanBuddy. In the last two options, the host arranges the ticket, meets you before the game and explains the cheering. The answers below cover each question visitors usually ask before going.

## Choosing how to go

### What are my options for going with a local?

| Option | Best for | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| A Korean friend or colleague | People who already know someone who supports a team | Who books the tickets |
| A marketplace host (e.g. Airbnb Experiences) | Travelers who want a guided night with reviews on the same platform | Venue for 2027, meal included or not, age limit |
| A local-buddy platform (e.g. HanBuddy) | Solo travelers and foreign residents who want a small group with a Korean buddy | Dates, what is included, cancellation terms |

### What do marketplace experiences look like?

Two Airbnb Experiences at Jamsil show the range. One includes a pre-game Korean fried chicken or pork BBQ meal, with tickets and a full meal provided by the host ([listing](https://www.airbnb.com/experiences/4217908)). Another is led by a host who describes being a former elite player and explains strategy and cheering during the game; guests pay for their own food ([listing](https://www.airbnb.com/experiences/7151199)). Both were built around Jamsil Baseball Stadium, which closed after 2026.

### How does HanBuddy work?

[HanBuddy](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/about) is a platform that connects travelers and foreign residents in Korea with local buddies. A buddy is a Korean local who hosts a small-group experience and goes with you. For a game night, the price includes the match ticket and stadium food, and you meet your buddy one hour before the game ([experience page](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/2)). Since June 2026 HanBuddy has run 8 game nights for 20 guests, with an average rating of 4.9 out of 5 from 15 guest surveys (June to October 2026).

## Season and stadiums

### When is the KBO season?

From spring to autumn. The 2026 season opened on March 28 with 10 clubs playing 144 games each ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/sports/20251219/2026-kbo-season-to-begin-march-28)). As of November 2026 there are no games until next season, and the 2027 opening date had not been announced at the time of writing.

### Which stadiums are in Seoul?

- **Jamsil Olympic Stadium (temporary ballpark):** home of the LG Twins and Doosan Bears for 2027–2031, converted to about 18,000 seats ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/sports/20240709/lg-twins-and-doosan-bears-to-temporarily-play-at-olympic-main-stadium-for-5-seasons)).
- **Gocheok Sky Dome:** home of the Kiwoom Heroes in Guro-gu, a domed ballpark that opened in 2015 and seats 16,744 for baseball ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gocheok_Sky_Dome)).

### What happened to Jamsil Baseball Stadium?

It hosted its last regular-season game, Doosan against Lotte, on October 12, 2026 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/sports/twins-bears-to-meet-in-kbo-wild-card-in-battle-of-jamsil-rivals/12917467)). Built in 1982, the KBO's first season, it is being replaced by a 30,000-seat dome due in March 2032 ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260403/historic-seoul-stadium-to-host-kbo-all-star-game-in-final-season)). The area is reached from Sports Complex Station on Subway Lines 2 and 9.

### What if it rains?

Open-air games can be called off for rain. Gocheok Sky Dome has a roof, so it is the safer pick in the rainy season.

## Tickets

### How do I buy KBO tickets myself?

Through the home club's official ticketing partner, linked from the club's website. For the 2026 season, LG Twins home tickets were sold on Ticketlink from 11:00 seven days before each game, up to 8 per person, with a 1,000 won fee per ticket. LG's page lists a Ticketlink Global call center (1644-3850) for foreign buyers ([LG Twins](https://www.lgtwins.com/ticket/general)). Recheck for 2027, as partners and rules can change.

### Do I need a ticket if I go with a host?

Usually not. Both Airbnb listings above provide tickets, and HanBuddy includes the match ticket in the price ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)).

## Cheering culture

### How does cheering work at a KBO game?

The home crowd follows a cheer captain and a small cheerleading squad, and each batter has a personal fight song sung as the batter steps up ([Wikipedia: Baseball cheering culture in South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_cheering_culture_in_South_Korea)). Koreans call these songs *eungwon-ga*. Each club has one captain and four or five cheerleaders (same source).

### What are thundersticks?

Inflatable balloon sticks that fans bang together. The LG Twins were the first club in the world to introduce them (same source).

### Do teams have their own traditions?

Yes. Lotte Giants fans, for example, cheer with newspapers and orange plastic bags (same source).

### Do I need to learn the songs first?

No. A local next to you can teach the short parts as each player comes up. HanBuddy buddies teach the cheering songs during the game, and no Korean or baseball knowledge is needed ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)).

## On the day

### What should I eat?

The classic is *chimaek*, fried chicken and beer (Wikipedia, above). If you have dietary restrictions and book with HanBuddy, write them under Special Requests and your buddy plans the food around them.

### How long does a game last?

About three hours, according to one Jamsil host ([Airbnb listing](https://www.airbnb.com/experiences/7151199)). HanBuddy's schedule allows about 180 minutes for the game after an hour of meeting, entry and food.

### Can I go alone?

Yes. On HanBuddy, guests come solo, as couples and with friends, and everyone sits together. Game nights are for guests aged 19 and older.

### How do booking and cancellation work on HanBuddy?

Pay online with PayPal, or message HanBuddy for another method such as a Korean bank transfer. Bookings close 24 hours before the start. After buddy approval, the refund is full at least 48 hours before the start, 50% from 24 to under 48 hours, and none within 24 hours; if the buddy cancels, the refund is full ([FAQ](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/faq)).

[Open-Air KBO Baseball Night](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/2) and [Indoor Dome KBO Baseball Night](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/activities/1). The open-air listing was built around Jamsil Baseball Stadium, so check the page for the 2027 venue and dates.
