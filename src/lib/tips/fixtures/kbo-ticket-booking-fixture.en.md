## Before you book

Tickets for most games go on sale a few days ahead. Pick the home team's official ticketing page and check the game date first.

| Seat area | Good for             |

| Infield   | Seeing every pitch   |
| Outfield  | Cheering with locals |

## On game day

- Bring your passport or the booking number.
- Arrive 30 minutes early. See the [HanBuddy experiences](https://hanbuddy.kr/en/explore) for a guided visit.
